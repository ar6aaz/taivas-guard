CHANGES (2026-11-07)

Rewritten in Python for IFIC Guard, so that it runs without MATLAB or Octave.
- The 24 field-strength tables were extracted unchanged into p1546_tables.json.
- Annex 5 s.4.2, E': both logarithms now have the same base, as the Recommendation
  states. Upstream divides log10(h1/10) by the natural log of 2.
- Annex 5 s.8: on a mixed path with h1 below 3 m, Esea now uses 3 m.
- An all-sea path with h1 from 1 m to 3 m is computed at h1 with a warning, rather
  than raised to 3 m.
- Inputs outside the valid range raise an error rather than being clamped.
- Removed: location variability (q other than 50%), log files, plotting and profile
  reading. The search routine was replaced with a standard-library bisection.
